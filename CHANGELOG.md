# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-22)

First public release.

- `SKILL.md`: method, catalogue of the 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 hints with solution methods, recognition guide, code tables, public-transport tips and pitfalls.
- `scripts/jh.py`: RD ↔ WGS84, projection, Kenny, 7-segment, Caesar, letter values, digital root, binary, Dutch number words, anagrams, image diffs, symbol grids and speech transcription.
- `references/`: AI evaluation of the 2023, 2024 and 2026 hints. These are the AI's own plausibility judgements, not official scores.
